  > *"Let's jump into the live simulation. Alex, our AI interviewer, speaks through our sub-500ms neural voice stream. Listen to the question Alex asks:"*

  > *"I see you used Redis in your e-commerce project for high-throughput caching. Walk me through your cache invalidation strategy when a product goes out of stock."*

* **Presenter Spoken Dialogue:**
  > *"A monolithic AI prompt would say: 'Good answer, Redis is fast!' But watch the Dynamic Follow-Up Agent catch the omission in real time:"*
* **System Spoken Audio (Alex Follow-Up Probe):**
  > *"You mentioned using Redis for scalability, but what specific concurrency bottlenecks did you hit when testing product checkouts, and how did your cache handle sudden out-of-stock updates?"*
* **Presenter Spoken Dialogue:**
  > *"This is the breakthrough. The AI forces Rahul to move from textbook generalities to authentic architectural defense. If Rahul freezes for more than 4 seconds, our empathy scaffolding immediately steps in to reassure him without lowering evaluation standards."*
